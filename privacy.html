Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Accurate Appraisal Group, LLC (“we,” “us”) respects the privacy of visitors to this website and of people who contact us about appraisal services. This policy describes what this static site does and does not collect.

Who we are

Accurate Appraisal Group, LLC is a residential real estate appraisal practice based in Milford, Connecticut. This website is a static informational site. It is not hosted on WordPress.

Information we collect

This website does not use an account system, does not require cookies to display content, and does not include third-party advertising. We do not run analytics pixels by default.

If you use the inquiry form, your browser will open a draft email containing the name, email, phone, assignment type, and message you entered. That information is not stored on this website’s server because the site has no backend database. Once you send the email, the contents are handled as ordinary business correspondence.

If you later connect the form to a service such as Formspree, Netlify Forms, or a similar provider, that provider’s privacy policy will also apply. Update this page if you enable such a service.

How we use information you send us

Contact details and property notes are used only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a fee quote, schedule an inspection, complete an appraisal assignment, and meet legal or professional record-keeping duties that apply to licensed appraisers. We do not sell personal information.

Appraisal files

Information gathered for an appraisal assignment — including the property address, interior photographs, and intended-user details — is handled as part of the professional workfile. Retention and confidentiality of workfiles follow applicable law, client agreements, and USPAP record-keeping requirements, which are separate from this website policy.

Cookies and logs

The site itself does not set tracking cookies. The company that hosts the files (for example Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages, or another static host) may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and uptime. Review that host’s policy as well.

Third-party fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when the font files are requested. You can self-host the fonts and remove that request if you prefer.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging real estate valuation services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You may email us to ask what correspondence we hold from you, to correct an address or phone number, or to ask us to close an inquiry that did not become an assignment. Some appraisal workfile records must be kept for a required period and cannot be deleted on request.

Connecticut and other state privacy laws

A small residential appraisal practice operating a static brochure site generally falls outside the scope of comprehensive consumer privacy statutes that apply to large-scale commercial data processors. If that changes because of new law or a change in how this site collects data, we will update this policy.

Changes

We may revise this policy when the site’s technology or our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the inquiry form on the home page or to the business email you publish there. Add that address here before the site goes live.
