Why clients call us

Expertise you can schedule around.

Welcome to Accurate Appraisal Group, LLC, where we bring expertise and reliability to residential appraisals. Founded in 2008 in Norwalk, CT, we have a long record of serving clients with careful, timely work. In 2012 headquarters moved to Bossier City, LA. In 2021 we returned to Connecticut and now operate from Milford.

Our commitment to accurate and timely appraisals is reflected in an average turn time of 48 hours from the site inspection.

Who we work with

  • Homeowners buying, selling, refinancing, or removing PMI
  • Attorneys handling estates, divorce, and litigation support
  • CPAs and tax professionals who need a supportable value
  • Appraisal management companies that need reliable Connecticut coverage
