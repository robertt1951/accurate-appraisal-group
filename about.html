About Accurate Appraisal Group

A Connecticut residential appraisal practice built around independent analysis, clear reports, and respect for your timeline.

Our history

From Norwalk to Milford — with a stop in Louisiana.

Accurate Appraisal Group, LLC was founded in 2008 in Norwalk, Connecticut. From the beginning the work has been residential valuation: producing dependable opinions of value that banks, attorneys, homeowners, and management companies can actually use.

In 2012 the headquarters moved to Bossier City, Louisiana. In 2021 the practice returned to Connecticut and is now based in Milford.

That path matters because it is the same practice, the same standard of work, and a deeper view of how different markets price housing. What has not changed is the promise on the current site: accurate and on time.

The appraiser

Lisa D. Tarver

Lisa D. Tarver is a Connecticut Certified Residential Real Estate Appraiser (credential RCR.0002070). She is the name associated with Accurate Appraisal Group’s public listings and the person clients have worked with across the firm’s Connecticut years.

Residential appraisal is a licensed profession. Reports are developed to professional standards, including the Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice (USPAP), and the appraiser’s job is an independent opinion — not an advocate’s number.

How we work

  • Confirm intended use, property type, and deadline before the inspection is set.
  • Inspect the property, measure, photograph, and note condition and amenities.
  • Research market data, comparables, and public records.
  • Reconcile the approaches to value that apply to the assignment.
  • Deliver a written report you and your intended users can stand behind.
What we stand for

Accuracy is not a slogan. It is the product.

Clients hire an appraiser when a number has consequences: a loan, a settlement, a tax position, an estate, or a private sale. We treat that number as a professional opinion supported by market evidence, not a guess and not a negotiation.

Independent

No interest in the sale price, the loan amount, or the outcome of a dispute. The assignment is the value opinion.

Timely

Average turn time of 48 hours from the site inspection, with communication if a property or data issue will change that.

Local

Connecticut housing is not generic. Neighborhood, school district, water, and condo association details change value.
